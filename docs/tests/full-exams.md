## How the mock exams work

- **Countdown:** 120 minutes begin when you click **Start full mock**, continue across page reloads, and trigger automatic submission at zero.
- **Navigation:** jump to any numbered question, move forward or back, and flag items you want to review.
- **Answer selection:** questions indicate whether one or several options must be selected.
- **Scoring:** answers are hidden until submission. Results show a percentage, unanswered questions, a breakdown by official exam domain, and rationales.
- **Retakes:** start a fresh attempt after finishing; the question and answer order changes. Attempts are saved only in your browser.

!!! warning "Independent practice, not an official simulation"
    The question bank contains **original educational questions**, not confidential or official exam items. We score all 70 questions in the newer mock exam because the real examination's ten unscored items are not identified. We cannot reproduce Anthropic's scaled scoring (passing score 720/1,000) or certify that a practice score corresponds to a pass.
